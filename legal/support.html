Frequently asked questions

A question or answer is wrong

The questions are generated by an AI and it sometimes gets things wrong. Write to us with what you saw (the grade, the topic and the question) and we will review it.

How do I delete my account or my data?

See the account deletion guide.

What data about my child does Tudy store?

Their nickname and grade, plus your sessions. The nickname is never sent to the AI. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

I reached the session limit

There is a maximum number of sessions per child in each 24-hour window. Once the time has passed you can request another.
