Delete your Tudy account and data

Tudy by Mayonesso

Deleting your account deletes all the data linked to it (listed below). You do not need to make a separate request for your data. If you want only part of it removed and to keep your account, for example the text of a request, say so in the same email and tell us what to remove.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address linked to your account to supermayonesso@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Tudy account". If your account is anonymous and has no email, tell us in the message how to identify it (for example, your child's nickname and grade). We will confirm when it is done.

What is kept

  • The questions in Tudy's general bank, which are based on the curriculum and are not tied to any person.
  • Technical logs (model used, timings, tokens, cost), unlinked from any person and from your text.
  • Whatever a provider already holds for its own retention period (for example, up to 30 days at Anthropic or 55 days at Google to detect abuse). Tudy cannot delete it sooner; see the Privacy Policy.
Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription made through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in the store's settings so you are not charged again.
