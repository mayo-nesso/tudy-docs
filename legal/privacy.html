Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-beta-3 (draft) · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Summary. Tudy is an app for mothers, fathers and guardians who study with their primary-school children. We store very little: your child's nickname and grade, and the text you write to request a session. That text is deleted after 12 months. There is no advertising and no usage analytics. We do not sell data.

1. Who is responsible

Tudy, by Mayonesso. Data controller: Sergio Mayorga, an individual, Chile. Contact for any data matter: supermayonesso@gmail.com.

2. Who the app is for

Tudy is for people 18 years or older who are a mother, father or guardian. Your child does not use the app alone and has no account of their own. We do not ask the child for data; only what you tell us about them.

We do not ask for location, contacts or photos. We ask for microphone and speech-recognition access only if you use dictation: your voice is turned into text on your phone, and the audio is neither sent nor stored. If your phone cannot do this offline, dictation is turned off and you can type instead. We use no advertising SDKs and no usage analytics.

4. Your text and personal data

Before your text is sent to a provider and before it is stored, an automatic step replaces personal data (names, national ID numbers (RUT), phone numbers, email addresses, web addresses) with markers such as [NAME]. Only the replaced text is sent and stored. This step is not perfect: an uncommon or misspelled name, a school or a piece of health information may get through. That is why we ask you not to write anyone's names or health data.

6. Error reports

If Tudy's server fails, we send a technical report so we can fix it. It does not include the child's name, what you write or your email. The server sends these reports, not the app, and they can be sent even before you accept this policy (for example, if the server fails while starting).

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correct it, delete it, object to a use, request a copy in a portable format and withdraw your consent at any time (Chile's Law 21.719; the GDPR if you are in the European Union). Write to us at supermayonesso@gmail.com from an address we can link to your account. We reply within the deadlines set by law. To delete your account, see the deletion guide. You can also complain to the competent data protection authority.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest on the provider's infrastructure. Each family can only see its own data. No system is infallible; if a breach that affects you occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Changes

If we change something important (a new provider, another kind of data), we raise the version of this policy and ask you to accept it again in the app.
